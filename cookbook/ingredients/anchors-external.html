External Hyperlinks

When you click on a hyperlink, you will be taken off the current page and navigated to a new page. External hyperlinks take you off the current website and onto a web page on a completely different site.

Hyperlinking to pages on a different site

If you want to create a hyperlink that goes to a page on an entirely different site, you can just provide the href attribute with the URL that you want to go to, like this: href="https://www.first.edu"

Bonus Tip: Use the target attribute

If you're navigating a user off of your site, it is generally good practice to open that page in another tab. You can do so by including a rather strange looking attribute and value pair: target="_blank"
